## Proposal Tone Rules
- Write in English unless the job post is clearly Vietnamese.
- Be short, direct, practical, and specific.
- Do not open with "Dear hiring manager" or "I hope this finds you well".
- Start with the client's problem or desired result.
- Mention one relevant project only, not the whole portfolio.
- Suggest a concrete implementation stack or execution plan.
- Keep the proposal around 80 to 120 words.
- End with one useful question that invites a reply.

## Pricing Hints
- Landing page: suggest a fixed price when scope is clear.
- Admin dashboard / CRUD backend: price higher than a simple landing page because of data modeling, auth, testing, and deployment.
- Scraping / automation: ask about target site, volume, login requirements, anti-bot protection, output format, and update frequency before final pricing.
